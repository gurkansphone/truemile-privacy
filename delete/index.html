TrueMile EV — Delete Your Account & Data

TrueMile EV is built and operated by Gate Engineering LLC. This page explains how to delete your account and data, exactly what is deleted, and the small vehicle-only record that can be retained. The full privacy policy is at gurkansphone.github.io/truemile-privacy.

Delete your account (in the app)

  1. Open TrueMile EV and tap the Settings gear.
  2. Tap My Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion runs immediately and is permanent — there is no recovery or grace period.

Can't sign in, or already uninstalled the app?

Email gatesoftwaredev@gmail.com from the email address your account uses, with the subject "Delete my TrueMile EV account". The deletion is performed for you, with a confirmation reply when done.

What is deleted, and what can be retained

Deleted immediately with your accountRetained
Your sign-in identity (email, name) and account ID · every trip and its GPS route · charging sessions and their locations, including your home charger location · all prices, costs, and rates you entered · settings, saved trailers, and paired-adapter details · generated mileage reports · subscription records held by the app An anonymized vehicle service history, keyed only to the vehicle's VIN — like a service book that follows the car to its next owner: vehicle specs, day-level charging facts, battery state-of-health, diagnostic codes, and tire records. It has no fields for your name, email, account, locations, routes, prices, or notes, so it cannot be linked back to you. Don't want it kept? Email us the VIN before deleting your account and it is removed too.

Anonymous community price contributions (never account-linked; expire automatically within 14 days) and crash reports (identified only by a random install ID, never your account).

Data on the phone itself (local database, settings, logs) is removed by uninstalling the app.

Delete some data without closing your account

  • Individual trips: open the trip in the app and delete it — the cloud copy is removed as well.
  • Community price contributions: turn off in Settings → My Account at any time (past contributions are anonymous and expire on their own within 14 days).
  • Anything else (for example a specific charging session): email gatesoftwaredev@gmail.com from your account email and it is removed for you.
